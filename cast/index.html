Who they are

A strategic partner, not a reference

CAST is a UK registered charity that helps social impact organisations make the fullest use of digital and AI technologies — removing barriers, creating opportunities, and nurturing the conditions that enable charities to be as responsive and resilient as possible. All of their programmes are free of charge.

For the RNLI, CAST is not simply a useful website to bookmark. Their resources map directly onto every major workstream in this strategy, their AI Task Force provides the governance credibility the propensity model needs, and their Digital Leads Network is the right home for the RNLI's internal Linux champions during the OS migration.

🤝

Phase 1 action: establish CAST as a named strategic partner

The CDIO should contact CAST in Month 1 to establish a formal relationship — not as a user of free resources, but as a charity that can both benefit from and contribute to the sector's shared digital knowledge. The RNLI's scale, maritime expertise, and open data assets make it an attractive CAST partner. The relationship should be mutual.

Formal references

Three CAST resources to cite formally in the strategy

These three warrant being named explicitly in the RNLI CDIO strategy document — not as optional extras but as structural commitments.

The Digital Principles

Ten principles designed with charities, funders and agencies. Should sit alongside the RNLI's sovereignty doctrine as the governing design principles for all new service development. They are sector-standard, board-legible, and provide a common language for technology decisions across the organisation.

View principles ↗

The AI Experimentation Canvas

Mandatory first step before any new AI use case is approved for development — including the legacy propensity model, the incident demand prediction model, and any generative AI experiments. Available as a Miro board or printable. The Board-level AI governance resolution should reference this canvas explicitly.

Open canvas ↗

The Charity AI Task Force

The governance body the RNLI should formally join for the ethics and responsible use dimensions of its AI programme. Particularly important for the vulnerable donor intersection in the propensity model, and for any AI use touching people rescued at sea. Convened by CAST and Zoe Amar Digital.

Mutual benefit

What the RNLI brings to CAST, not just takes

🌊

The RNLI as a CAST contributor

CAST's Shared Digital Guides platform has 90+ entries — written by charities for charities. As the RNLI builds out its processes, it should document and contribute guides on: publishing open geospatial data under CC-BY, deploying CiviCRM for a distributed volunteer network, running a Linux migration at a multi-site charity, and using open-source BI (Superset) for fundraising analytics. These contributions position the RNLI as a digital sector leader, serve OCOP Goal 2.3 (next-generation relevance), and give back to the commons that the RNLI will draw from.

📡

The maritime digital gap in the sector

CAST's current resource library is strong on mainstream charity digital topics — CRM, digital inclusion, fundraising technology — but relatively thin on maritime, operational, and safety-critical contexts. The RNLI has a unique opportunity to extend the sector's shared knowledge into this space: maritime data standards, offshore communications resilience, sensor integration for operational charities, and the digital dimensions of life-safety operations. This is not charity digital as usual — and that distinctiveness is a contribution.
