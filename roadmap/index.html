24-Month Delivery Plan · Five Phases · Eight Streams

Delivery Roadmap

Process first, then technology · Simplified + Support · OCOP aligned

Governing principle

Process first, technology second

One Crew, One Plan is explicit: design processes first, then enhance with technology. No workstream begins with a technology decision. Every stream opens with discovery — mapping what currently exists, talking to people who use it, and understanding what genuinely needs to change before selecting a tool.

⚖️

The two delivery pillars

Simplified: processes and operations adopting industry standards to ensure everyone is safer. Support: services geared towards better-serving the needs of volunteers and lifesavers. Every technology investment must demonstrably serve one or both of these pillars. If it doesn't, it doesn't proceed.

Fleet technology sequencing

Shore first, vessel second

🛥️

Honouring the OCOP timeline

One Crew, One Plan places "enhanced sensors and technology introduced on our lifeboats" in the 2030–2035 window, aligned with the new coastal lifeboat class arriving in 2028. This roadmap respects that sequencing. The first 24 months build the shore infrastructure — FreeTAK, comms mesh, shore-station data pipelines, the analytics platform — so that when vessel-level sensors are ready, the receiving infrastructure already exists and is proven. Don't build the vessel layer before you've built the shore layer that makes it useful.
