The income problem

When 66% of income comes from legacies

The RNLI's income profile is unlike most charities. 66% of net income arrives via legacy gifts — from people who are no longer alive. This shapes everything: the analytics must model three parallel pipelines simultaneously, not just the current-donor health dashboard that most charities build.

⚠️

The Fundraising Regulator signal

A 2025 Fundraising Regulator investigation found gaps in how RNLI supporter experience staff document conversations — specifically around vulnerable donors, large one-off gifts, and Power of Attorney situations. This points to a CRM data model that does not adequately capture the qualitative context of donor interactions. Any analytics build must start with fixing this underlying data quality gap. The vulnerable donor flag must be a structured field, not a free-text note.

Three pipelines

The analytics must model three things simultaneously

Pipeline 1: Current donor health

Real-time view of regular giving — active donors, lapsed donors by recency band, average gift trends, Gift Aid claim rate, campaign response by segment. Monthly update to executive. Standard but must be built on sovereign infrastructure (PostgreSQL + Superset), not Salesforce or Raiser's Edge in US-jurisdiction cloud.

Retention ratesGift AidSuperset

Pipeline 2: Legacy intent propensity

The highest-value investment. A machine learning model trained on historical data — which characteristics of committed supporters predicted eventual legacy notification? Output: a ranked list of supporters for legacy team outreach, refreshed quarterly. Built with XGBoost on UK infrastructure. No donor data sent to third-party AI APIs.

XGBoostSHAP explainabilityQuarterly refresh

Pipeline 3: Next-generation acquisition

Where are new supporters under 45 coming from? What converts them? What retains them? Open data interactions — developers using the API, journalists citing RNLI data, students using incident data — are potential acquisition signals that current analytics does not track. Directly addresses OCOP Goal 2.3.

The propensity model

Legacy intent scoring: how it works

🤖

Architecture: XGBoost, not a black box

The recommended model is XGBoost (gradient boosted decision trees). These produce interpretable feature importance scores, handle missing data gracefully — common in CRM records — work well on small-to-medium datasets (a few thousand known legacy outcomes), and do not require sending any donor data to a third-party API. The model runs entirely on RNLI UK infrastructure. Every score is accompanied by SHAP values — the specific features that drove that supporter's score — so the legacy team sees human-readable explanations, not opaque numbers.

💡

The counterintuitive finding: lapse-and-return is a positive signal

Supporters who lapse from regular giving after a long tenure and then re-engage have elevated legacy propensity. The hypothesis: lapsing may coincide with a life change — retirement, health event, bereavement — that prompts a review of finances and charitable commitments, including Will-writing. Treating these supporters as lost is a significant missed opportunity. The model explicitly captures this pattern.

Sovereign technology stack

CiviCRM and the fundraising platform

CiviCRM — core CRM

Open-source nonprofit CRM. UK-hostable. Gift tracking, legacy module, Gift Aid integration, campaign management. Used by Amnesty International, Greenpeace, and UK government bodies. Active UK community. Replaces Raiser's Edge / Salesforce NPSP.

Open sourceUK-hostableLegacy moduleGift Aid

Apache Superset — analytics

Self-hosted BI dashboards connecting directly to CiviCRM's PostgreSQL database. Donor pipeline dashboards, retention funnels, legacy propensity outputs, campaign performance — all in one UK-sovereign interface. Replaces Power BI.

Open sourcePostgreSQL nativeUK-hostable

Python + XGBoost — propensity

Legacy intent model built in Python. Trained on RNLI's own historical data. Runs on UK infrastructure. No donor data sent to US AI APIs. Model outputs written back to CiviCRM as supporter scores. Replaces Blackbaud AI / Salesforce Einstein.

XGBoostOn-premiseNo data leakage

Mautic — email & automation

Open-source marketing automation. UK-hostable. CiviCRM integration. Supporter journey automation, personalised campaign triggers, Gift Aid reminder sequences, legacy cultivation journeys. Replaces Mailchimp / Dotdigital.

Open sourceCiviCRM integrationUK-hostable

Stripe UK + GoCardless — payments

Stripe UK Ltd (UK-registered, UK data processing) for card donations. GoCardless (UK-founded, UK-regulated) for Direct Debit regular giving. Both integrate with CiviCRM. Lower fees than JustGiving for direct giving.

Ethics & governance

Propensity scores rank intent likelihood — they do not authorise aggressive outreach. Any high-scoring supporter flagged as potentially vulnerable triggers mandatory review before contact. Board governance resolution required. Annual independent model audit. GDPR profiling disclosure in privacy notice.
