The France precedent

This is now a proven path

In April 2026, France's DINUM formally mandated Linux across all government desktops — an estimated 2.5 million workstations. The French Gendarmerie's GendBuntu deployment reached 97% of its 103,000 workstations by 2024, saving approximately €2m per year in licensing and reducing total cost of ownership by 40%. Germany's Schleswig-Holstein completed nearly 80% of its 30,000-workstation migration by early 2026, recording €15m in savings.

🛡️

This is a crew safety decision

The case for Linux is not primarily about cost. RNLI volunteers involved in Channel rescue operations have been named online and physically targeted by hostile actors. The digital dimension of this threat — crew PII in US-jurisdiction cloud, operational patterns in proprietary telemetry, donor data as ransomware leverage — is real. A UK-sovereign, Linux-based estate has a materially smaller attack surface, no extraterritorial data jurisdiction, and no Microsoft telemetry leaking operational patterns to third parties.

Training and support

The volunteer literacy challenge

50,000+ volunteers use RNLI systems with varying digital literacy. The migration must be designed with volunteers, not for them.

LPI Linux Essentials

Industry-standard Linux certification. Foundation level — appropriate for all staff. UK training partners deliver this. RNLI should target LPI certification for every IT staff member and one Linux champion per shore station by Phase 5.

LPIUK trainingFoundation level

Linux champion network

One trained Linux champion per station — the local go-to person for questions during migration. Recruited from existing volunteer digital enthusiasts. Supported by the CAST Digital Leads Network. Cost: time, not money.

1 per stationCAST networkPeer support

90-day parallel running

No big-bang cutover. Every migrated workstation runs alongside Windows for 90 days. Staff choose when they're ready to switch fully. The France/Gendarmerie model: build internal competence before mandating, not the reverse.
